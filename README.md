## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧾 ClauseWise — AI-Powered Contract Intelligence

An intelligent contract analysis platform designed to simplify complex legal agreements. ClauseWise helps users understand legal documents through plain-English explanations, risk identification, obligation highlighting, and AI-generated summaries.

**Tech Stack:** `React` `Tailwind CSS` `Flask` `Python` `BERT` `NLP`

### 🏗️ SiteOps — Intelligent Construction Operations

A smart construction workforce management platform focused on operational transparency. Features include geofenced attendance, facial verification, wage tracking, material inventory management, and workforce monitoring.

**Tech Stack:** `Flutter` `FastAPI` `Python` `SQLite` `OpenCV` `Geofencing`

### 🌡️ ThermaCity — Urban Heat Intelligence

An urban heat analysis and mitigation planning platform focused on Mumbai. Combines satellite imagery, thermal data visualization, machine learning analytics, and interactive maps to identify urban heat hotspots and support climate-resilient planning.

**Tech Stack:** `React` `FastAPI` `Python` `Machine Learning` `GIS` `Remote Sensing`

### 🎯 SkillOrbit — AI Recruitment Intelligence

An AI-driven recruitment platform that analyzes candidate profiles, matches applicants against job requirements, ranks candidates using machine learning, and provides explainable hiring insights.

**Tech Stack:** `React` `FastAPI` `Python` `Machine Learning` `NLP` `Vector Search`

### 📚 Skillo — Personalized Learning Platform

An intelligent learning and skill development platform designed to help students and professionals discover learning paths, track progress, and build industry-relevant skills through personalized recommendations and learning analytics.

**Tech Stack:** `React` `FastAPI` `Python` `AI` `Learning Analytics` `Recommendation Systems`
